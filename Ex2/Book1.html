Ghosts of the Silent Hills

Overview:

"Ghosts of the Silent Hills" is a horror anthology that immerses readers in eerie, supernatural experiences set in the dense, silent forests of the Himalayas, particularly around Shimla and Mashobra.The book captures the chilling atmosphere of isolated hills, where readers may encounter spectral figures, hear unexplained screams, or witness other paranormal phenomena.

Author Background:

Anita Krishan, born in Shimla in 1955, spent her early years in the Himalayan town, earning a master’s degree in English literature from Himachal University . She has had a long career as an educator and is a versatile writer, exploring genres from historical fiction to paranormal stories.

Structure and Stories

The book is divided into two parts, each containing five stories, totaling ten haunting tales. The stories often revolve around restless spirits or dissatisfied souls, reflecting societal flaws and human emotions. For example, the first story, “An Uncanny Attachment”, recounts incidents at a Gram Sevak Training Centre in Mashobra during the early 1950s, involving mysterious events experienced by the author’s family and staff.
